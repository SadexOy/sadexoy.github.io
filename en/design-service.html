Customer experience: Rakennusliike J. Malm

Sampo School – an enclosed rainwater system

Since 1978, Rakennusliike J. Malm from Pirkanmaa has provided high-quality solutions for housing construction, service building construction and contracting. One of the company's newest projects is the Sampo school and day-care centre in Kaleva, Tampere, whose rainwater systems were designed and built together with Sadex.

Sadex experts were involved in designing this unique project from the very start. With iron-clad expertise, an impressive and functional result was achieved while saving costs.
