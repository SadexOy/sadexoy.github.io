The Sadex installation service

The passion of four brothers is the hallmark of Sadex, and it has spread to everyone at Sadex – we work as if it were our own home. We design and install more than 7,300 projects a year, but we never take on work at the expense of quality.

Our NPS, which measures customer satisfaction, is 87 (06/2026), which speaks of very successful projects. Our most important goal is a satisfied customer and a safe property.

  • Our installers, authorised by the manufacturer, fit the products in line with the regulations without damaging the structures.

  • We install all products cut to length, avoiding unnecessary joints and therefore the risk of leaks.

  • A working whole consists of many important details, even small ones, which our installers take care of with years of experience.

  • We give a five-year installation warranty on our installation work, in addition to the 15–50-year product warranty. Read more about our warranty.

  • We take occupational safety seriously. Our installers always wear safety equipment, and installations are carried out with an aerial work platform in line with the regulations.

The whole process went well and quickly, from the quote to the delivery. The installer had the right, professional approach to the job. Thank you!

– Leo Halonen

Our installers can install all our products all year round, even in challenging winter conditions (in Finnish). Our installers, who are not put off by wind or snowstorms, also clear any snow that the installation requires, at no extra cost. Our Finnish quality products are among the best on the market. They are made by Piristeel Oy, Finland’s largest manufacturer of rainwater systems, roof safety products and ladders.

The Sadex installation path: this is how easy it is!

1. Quick response

Contact us using the form and order a free installation survey for any building. Contacting us does not commit you to anything. You can order the survey and the installation at any time of year.

2. Careful survey

We make a free site survey at a time agreed with you, or, if needed, we assess the building from photos.

3. Clear quote

You receive a plan and a realistic assessment of the need to renew your rainwater system or roof safety equipment, the price and the options for carrying out the work.

4. Flexible scheduling

We move on to the installation. Our expert installs your gutters at a time that suits you.

5. Comprehensive warranty

Once the work is done, you can enjoy worry-free gutters and roof safety products for decades to come! Our products come with up to a 5-year installation warranty and a 15–50-year product warranty. Read more about our warranty.
