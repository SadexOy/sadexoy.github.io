Inspection of roof safety products

The required annual inspection – keeping roof access routes and residents safe

CE marking is an EU-wide standardisation. All CE-marked products related to personal safety need a specified level of corrosion protection and an annual inspection. We inspect your roof as a certified operator.

On this page you can read about the requirements, execution and pricing of inspections, and send a quote request or a callback request.

CE-marked safety products require an annual inspection

Uninspected products cannot be relied on and must not be used as safety products. Sadex offers a certified inspection service for all CE-marked products – on every kind of building.

We stand behind our work, so in addition to the CE safety products the inspection also covers all roof safety products installed by Sadex.

On your roof we inspect:

  • Snow guards
  • Wall ladders
  • Roof ladders
  • Fire escape ladders
  • Roof walkways
  • Climbing rails
  • Cable systems
  • Anchor points
  • Horizontal rails

Roof walkways, roof ladders, climbing rails and SafeLine sliding anchors require an annual CE inspection.

An inspection keeps your roof and the surroundings of your property safe

We inspect the CE safety products on your roof and at the same time all roof safety products installed by Sadex, such as snow guards, wall ladders, roof ladders, fire escape ladders, roof walkways, climbing rails, cable systems, anchor points and horizontal rails.

Type approvals and certificates of Sadex roof safety products:

  • CE marking certificate: roof walkways, roof ladders, climbing rails and SafeLine sliding anchors.
  • Eurofins verification certificate: wall ladders, snow guards and horizontal rails.
  • EU type examination (SGS Fimko): climbing rails.

Inspections are not estimates: we inspect reliably, with a guarantee

We inspect every CE-marked product, and at the same time we check the general condition of all the products we have installed and carry out the necessary tightening.

The inspection includes making sure that all the products on the roof are:

  • Compliant with the requirements
  • Safe and durable in their general condition
  • Correctly positioned
  • Correctly sized
  • Correctly fixed

Among other things, we check the fixing spacing of the product brackets, the tightness and condition of the bolts, and the degree of any bending and rusting.

After the inspection you receive an inspection report that lists the findings and clear deficiencies with photos and text, so you know what corrective measures, if any, are needed for the products. The inspector can also make a general sizing and a proposal for new product needs and deficiencies.

You also gain certainty that the products on your property meet the regulations, are genuinely safe and are correctly sized and fixed. What you are left with is a clear report, peace of mind and the certainty that your property is safe for residents and for anyone going up on the roof.
