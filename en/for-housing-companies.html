A safe, weatherproof roof for every housing company

The roof is your housing company's most important structure – under it, buildings become homes. Your housing company is responsible for enabling safe living in all conditions – literally under the roof and around it.

We are your partner in everything to do with roof safety and rainwater drainage. We make sure your roof is safe during heavy rain, under the feet of the snow clearer and the chimney sweep, for the gutter cleaner and when the emergency exit route is the only option.

We provide rainwater and roof safety solutions for every kind of property. Our customers range from new buildings to housing companies that are decades old – from terraced houses to apartment buildings and architecturally significant buildings. In full renovations we also work as a subcontractor, in partnership with construction companies.

But our services go much further than big projects and major renovations. Small mishaps happen from time to time – perhaps the gutters took a knock or the roof safety equipment needs straightening. We have the ability and the will to fix all the smaller problems too. Together we keep your property in good condition, taking care of big and small things alike!

Need help with your housing company's roof safety? This is how it works

Is the roof leaking, are the gutters dented, or do you doubt that the snow guards will withstand the winter's snow loads? Would you like a professional to check whether your roof safety has been implemented in line with the latest regulations? Whether your issue is big or small, we will take it on.

1. Free site survey

Everything starts with a survey. Contact us and we will agree on a time for our representative to survey the condition of your roof safety and drainage on site. You will find out the real condition of your roof safety and what kind of life you can expect from the current system. We survey everything related to rainwater and roof safety, so don't hesitate to contact us.

2. A clear quote

You quickly receive a written, binding quote from us. The quote clearly specifies the required measures, the content of the installation work and the schedules, among other things. We go through the content of the quote with you point by point.

3. Presentation to your housing company's decision-makers

If you wish, we present our plan to your housing company's decision-makers, for example at a board meeting or the general meeting.

4. The work done right in one go

When it is time to get to work, our installers carry out the job from start to finish on the agreed schedule. During the renovation you can live in the housing company as normal. We inform your residents about the work, our work poses no danger to bystanders, and we clean up when we leave. We keep you informed of the stages of the work and you also receive photos of the finished building, so you stay up to date on the progress of the project.

5. Support after the project too

We take care of roof matters after the renovation and installation work too, helping with things such as maintaining your roof safety equipment and servicing the gutters. We also provide the annual inspections required by the regulations for certain roof safety products, such as the anchor points for safety harnesses and ropes.

Roof maintenance and inspection services

What looks fine from the ground may in fact need prompt maintenance or be a safety risk. A roof condition inspection makes sure the building's most important protection is in order, and maintenance services extend the life and performance of the roof.

Rainwater system cleaning

Cleaning the gutters is a small job that can prevent major damage. When the rainwater system works, the water is led away in a controlled way, where it should go. When a gutter is blocked, the system can be damaged, especially in winter, and the water can find its way into the plinth, the facade or the roof structures.
