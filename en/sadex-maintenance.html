Sadex Huolto keeps your roof in good condition and helps prevent costly damage

Roof maintenance and inspection services protect your building and extend the life of your roof.

The annual inspections of roof safety products are often left undone, even though they are vital for the safety of the property. We make sure these inspections happen, and at the same time we check the condition of the roof and carry out the necessary maintenance – even in one efficient visit.

We know what roof maintenance and safety require. We offer inspection and maintenance services for detached houses, housing companies, businesses and property owners.

Let's avoid major renovations together

We maintain and inspect so that your roof lasts.

Regular inspections and maintenance ensure the longest possible life for the roof. At the same time, roof safety is checked so that working on the roof and moving around the yard are safe in every situation. That is why many manufacturers of roof safety products require annual inspections to guarantee that their products work properly.

We do not inspect roofs to find renovation work for ourselves; we always act in the property owner's interest. Every inspection and maintenance visit is an investment in the condition of the roof, but if there is nothing on the roof that needs maintenance, that is the best possible outcome for everyone.

We are here to help – whether it is commercial premises, a housing company, a detached house or a cottage!

Frequently asked questions

Who is the service for?

The maintenance service suits:

  • People living in detached houses: peace of mind, no surprises, postponing an expensive renovation through maintenance.
  • Housing companies and property managers: easy budgeting, a lighter burden of responsibility for the board, a long-term maintenance plan (PTS).
  • Businesses and property owners: preserving the value of the property, ensuring safety as required by law.
What if faults are found on the roof?

You can have the repair and maintenance work done either by us or, using the report we prepare, by another roof maintenance company.

If you wish, you can authorise us when ordering the condition inspection to take care of small maintenance work during the inspection. For larger jobs we always give you a quote or a price estimate first.

Do I need to be present during the inspection?

No, as long as the inspector has free access to both the roof and the roof void (if it is possible to move around in the roof void at all). We always let you know in advance when we are coming.

What kinds of roofs do you inspect?

We inspect almost all types of roofs: tiled roofs, sheet metal roofs, standing seam roofs and felt roofs.

Can you get the household tax credit for maintenance?

Yes, you can get the Finnish household tax credit (kotitalousvähennys) for maintenance. The invoice always shows the labour share separately, which you can report to the Tax Administration to get the credit.

Which areas do you cover?

Our service areas are Southern Finland, Central Finland, the Oulu region, Pirkanmaa, Varsinais-Suomi and Savo. We carry out the inspection within three weeks of the order.
