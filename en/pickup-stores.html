Take gutters home straight away from our stores

Ladders, roof safety products and gutters as supplies all over Finland

A helpful supplies store!

When you want to install gutters or snow guards yourself, our stores stock the quietest rainwater systems on the market, the safest ladders, snow guards and other roof safety products for every roof. We help you find the best solutions, whether for a detached house, a cottage or even a garage. Order gutters in advance cut to exactly the length you need and can transport – forget ready-made three-metre pieces!

You can check our product range in our online shop (in Finnish) – products marked with a 3–7 working day delivery time can be picked up from our stores straight away, without an advance order.

The range in the other stores varies by location, so we recommend calling first to check availability. In the store you can conveniently pay by card. If you want to be sure of a quick visit, order the products in advance from the online shop and you can pick up your order without unnecessary waiting – with the best tips thrown in! Welcome!
