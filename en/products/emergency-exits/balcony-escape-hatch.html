Escape hatch

The escape hatch is a safe, fast, type-approved safety product for emergency evacuation from balconies. Installed in the balcony floor, the hatch enables safe and fast emergency evacuation for all residents of the building – on every floor. With an escape hatch, evacuation is faster than with an aerial ladder truck, as residents can rescue themselves by climbing down from their balcony to the balcony below.

The standard size of the escape hatch is 620 x 620 mm.

An easy-to-use, maintenance-free escape hatch that lasts the whole life of the balcony

What sets this escape hatch apart from other evacuation hatches on the market is its sturdy, patented telescopic ladder, which is stable to climb down. The hatch is opened with an easy-to-use, sealed release handle underneath the hatch.

The ladder folds into a small space inside the hatch and conveniently extends downwards when the moment comes. On top of that, the escape hatch is completely maintenance-free and, under normal conditions, lasts the entire service life of the balcony. Thanks to high-quality materials, the hatch has excellent corrosion resistance whether or not the balcony is glazed.

Intended primarily for professional builders, for use on apartment-building balconies, the escape hatch ensures a safe exit for the residents of the flat and is otherwise unobtrusive on the balcony. It protects and secures without reducing the comfort of using the balcony and without the emergency exit being constantly on show. The escape hatch is easy to install, and the fire insulation wool placed inside the hatch ensures the hatch’s specified fire insulation performance.

The hatch has a large, easy-to-use access opening that can only be opened from the resident’s own balcony. Residents therefore need not worry about the security of their balcony. The escape hatch is sealed during installation, which prevents misuse.

An escape hatch that does not restrict use of the balcony

In addition to its patented ladder, this escape hatch stands out from other escape hatches for its usability. The hatch cover rises only 10 mm above the balcony surface, so light furniture can also be placed on top of the hatch.

The escape hatch therefore does not restrict the use of the balcony in any way: the balcony can be furnished as normal and the hatch can be walked on.

How does the escape hatch work?

The hatch is installed in an opening made in the balcony floor. In an emergency, the hatch cover is opened. The sealed release lever is turned 90°, which opens the hatch. The fire insulation wool on top of the lower hatch is pushed down. The telescopic ladder is then lifted from its bracket and allowed to descend. The ladder is now ready for use. The escape hatch can only be opened from the resident’s own balcony.

When does a balcony need an escape hatch?

A balcony escape hatch is the right choice of emergency exit route, for example, when:

  • The building’s fire safety regulations require it
  • There is no other direct exit from the balcony
  • The height or location of the structure calls for extra safety during evacuation

If you are unsure whether your building needs an escape hatch, Sadex’s experts will help you identify the requirements for your site.

Escape hatch features

VTT type approval and high fire resistance class

VTT has granted the escape hatch type approvals VTT-RTH-00016-16 and VTT-RTH-00017-16, and it meets fire resistance classes EI 120 and EI 60.

Material

The escape hatch’s surface material is galvanised steel and its top cover is aluminium. The hatch is powder coated. Hot-dip galvanised steel and powder coating give the escape hatch excellent corrosion protection.

Colour

Our standard colour for the escape hatch is:

  • RR21 light grey

The escape hatch is also available in special colours at extra cost.

As standard, the escape hatch comes in light grey RR21, and for an extra charge it can be finished in the colour of the customer’s choice, from standard colours to more special colour options. Sadex’s exceptionally wide range of standard colours ensures escape hatches that suit the facade – so where needed, the hatch can also be made to work well visually as part of the overall balcony design.

  • Standard escape hatch size 620 x 620 mm
