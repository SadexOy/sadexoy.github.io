Opening escape ladder

Fitted with a release-operated ladder section, the Opening escape ladder provides a reliable and stable emergency exit route. Thanks to their box-like casing, opening fire escape ladders are almost invisible on the facade. When the ladder is needed, it opens quickly and easily by pulling a pin at the top of the ladder. Conversely, unauthorised climbers and children cannot get onto the ladder from below. Opening fire escape ladders are ideal for buildings where a discreet result is wanted without compromising on safety.

Tested by VTT, the Opening escape ladder is suitable for use on all types of building, from small houses to apartment buildings and industrial halls. The grooved pattern on the rungs prevents slipping and ensures secure footing.

The Finnish National Rescue Association (SPEK) supports the use of opening ladders as emergency exit routes (statement 09/13). The Opening escape ladder is manufactured by the Finnish company PP-Tuote Oy.

Material

The Opening escape ladder is made from aluminium profile. The fixings are stainless steel.

Colour

Our standard colours for the Opening escape ladder are:

  • RR20 white
  • RR23 dark grey

The Opening escape ladder is also available in special colours at extra cost.

Technical data

  • Weight 2.5 kg/m
  • Height 3 m, 3.9 m, 4.8 m etc., in 0.9/3 m increments
  • Width 35 mm
  • Depth 75 mm
