Why is a rainwater system important for the well-being of your home?

Gutters have an important job: they lead the water that collects on the roof away from the walls, foundations and walkways of the building. It pays to be sure of the condition of your property’s rainwater system, because at worst an undersized or otherwise inadequate system can let water into the structures, causing moisture damage and related problems.

A rainwater system sized to the dimensions of your house

A correctly sized rainwater system avoids problems, and the gutter size should always be determined by the specific features of the roof and the type of property. If this is not taken into account, even a new rainwater system can be undersized. A gutter that is too small causes trouble: water overflows the gutter and soaks the foundations. A correctly sized gutter leads the water through the downpipe into the drain even in heavy rain.

Points to take into account:
• The volume, shape and width of the gutter for the building
• Sufficient falls in the gutters and the right number of downpipes in the right places
• The right number of rainwater drains and their location
• The roofing material, the roof area and the pitch of the roof

How do you know your rainwater system needs renewing?

Check your gutters regularly, especially in spring and autumn. Signs that renewal is needed:

  1. Sagging, rusty or cracked gutters or downpipes
  2. Water does not run down but overflows the gutters
  3. Downpipes leak or have partly come loose
  4. Standing water or growth in the gutters
  5. Moisture or mould damage appears in the structures

A rainwater system does not last forever. If the system is more than 20 years old, otherwise shows signs of ageing or has clear problems, it is time to consider renewing it. A free Sadex site survey helps to assess the situation on site!

Round or square rainwater system?

Whether a half-round or a square rainwater system is more pleasing to the eye is a matter of taste. Our half-round rainwater system is by far the most popular, and more than 90% of the systems Sadex supplies are round. A half-round system performs more efficiently and needs less maintenance: a round gutter cleans itself, whereas debris can collect in the corners of a square one.

The RT guidelines published by Rakennustieto also recommend a half-round gutter because of its corrosion resistance. A round downpipe bends neatly in every direction, while a square one is limited to four directions. Both gutters can be cleaned from the ground – but a half-round one needs it less often.

Frequently asked questions about rainwater systems

Is a rainwater system mandatory for every building?

A rainwater system is not required by law, but installing one is always recommended. Building regulations and local detailed plans may require rainwater and meltwater to be led away from the walls and foundations of a building in a controlled way. In practice every building benefits from a rainwater system, because it prevents moisture damage, wear to the yard and settling of the foundations.

How can I maintain my rainwater system myself?

Keep the gutters and downpipes clear of debris and leaves. Clean them at least twice a year, in spring and in autumn. At the same time, check that the gutters are firmly attached and intact and that nothing blocks the flow of water. In winter, make sure ice does not block the water. Regular cleaning and a visual inspection extend the life of the system considerably.

If you notice damage, small problems and leaks can often be repaired, for example with sealant or by replacing parts of the system, but with bigger problems it may be time to renew the system.

How do I know which rainwater system suits me best?

The right system depends on the shape and area of the roof and the location of the building. Most important is that the rainwater system is sized correctly for the property. A steep or large roof, for example, may need wider gutters and more downpipes.

In terms of appearance, the right rainwater system is largely a matter of taste. You can choose a round or a square system, but water flows more smoothly in a round gutter and a round gutter does not clog as easily.

A free Sadex site survey tells you which system suits your roof best.

Can I install a rainwater system myself or do I need a professional?

You can install a rainwater system yourself if you know what you are doing. Using a professional is still recommended: installation errors can lead to leaks, water running in the wrong direction or damage to the structures. Sadex professionals size, install and document the system safely and in line with the regulations.

Can a rainwater system be installed in winter?

Yes. Rainwater systems can be installed all year round. The advantage of winter installation is that installation queues are often shorter and schedules more flexible. Sadex installs rainwater systems in the winter season too.
