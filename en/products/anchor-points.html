Safety rope anchor points

A comprehensive range of tested anchor points that keep the roof safe during maintenance and repair work. By fitting your roof with a rope anchor, you make sure roof workers can work safely and you avoid situations with a risk of falling. A continuous, unbroken access route is the safest solution: the less a person on the roof has to move without being attached to a safety rope, the lower the risk of falling.

A safe access route on the roof starts with design

The design of safety rope anchor points depends on things such as the height of the building and the pitch and material of the roof, but also on the number and location of the items that need maintenance and, for example, whether snow has to be cleared. Safety rope anchor points should always be placed so that they form a safe route across the whole roof.

On buildings higher than 9 metres, safety rope anchor points are required by law, but for safety reasons they are recommended on every building. Sadex experts are happy to help design the access routes and anchor points for your building. When the route is safe, the work runs smoothly and the risks are minimised.
