Side ladders

Our durable, sturdy side ladders provide safe access to the roof and an alternative to conventional wall ladders. Side ladders are positioned on the wall so that you climb to the roof with your side facing the wall. Finnish production ensures the first-class quality and durability of the side ladders. The side ladders are made by Piristeel Oy.

Material

The side ladder components are made from hot-dip galvanised steel, and the ladders are finished with a baked powder coating. Hot-dip galvanised steel and baked powder coating give excellent corrosion protection.

Colour

Sadex’s exceptionally wide range of standard colours ensures side ladders that suit the façade of your house, at no extra cost. Our standard side ladder colours are:

  • RR11 pine green
  • RR20 white
  • RR21 light grey
  • RR22 grey
  • RR23 dark grey
  • RR2H3 anthracite
  • RR32 dark brown
  • RR33 black
  • RR29 red
  • RR750 brick red
  • Zn galvanised

The colour of side ladders is usually chosen to match the rainwater system. The colours of the roof and windows are also taken into account when choosing the ladder colour. We are happy to help you choose a suitable colour!

Side ladders are also available in special colours at extra cost.

Technical data

  • Frame: 1.25 mm thick steel
  • Frame size 25 x 45 mm
  • Rung diameter 25 mm
  • Clear width 400 mm
  • The basic frame lengths are 1.2 m, 1.8 m, 2.7 m, 3.3 m and 4.2 m

The frames can be joined to one another with a screw joint using a reducing section.
