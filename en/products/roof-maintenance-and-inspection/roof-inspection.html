Roof inspection

Do you know what condition your roof is in? A roof condition inspection lets you make sure that the most important protection of your building is in good order.

An impartial roof inspection gives a clear picture of the roof’s current condition. The condition of a roof cannot be reliably assessed from the ground, because leaking penetrations, damage to the underlay, shortcomings in roof safety products or drainage problems, for example, can go completely unnoticed. Inspection services are particularly suitable when you want to know the true condition of your roof, plan future maintenance or make sure that roof safety is in order.

What does an inspection include?

  • Roofing fixings: condition of screws and seals
  • Condition of the coating: wear, paint finish, rust; on tiled roofs, weathering and surface roughness
  • Dirt: loose debris, growth, discolouration
  • Tightness of penetrations: vent pipes, antennas, roof hatches, roof extract fans
  • Flashing fixings and water routing: edge flashings, ridge flashings, drip edges, wall upstand flashings
  • Roof safety products: snow guards, wall ladders, roof walkways, roof ladders, safety rope anchor points
  • Drainage (i.e. gutters, downpipes, roof drains): dirt, rust, holes, sealants, standing water, how the downpipes work, etc.
  • Roof space inspection (only if the roof space is accessible): looking for leaks, checking penetrations from below, insulation, condition of the roof boarding, condition of the underlay

Why order a roof condition inspection?

Extending the life cycle of the roof
Every roof eventually reaches the end of its life and has to be replaced. However, by monitoring the condition of the roof and carrying out preventive maintenance, the need for a roof renovation can be postponed, even by years.

Better information
The condition of a roof cannot be assessed from the ground, and a non-professional will not spot every defect even on the roof. A survey carried out by a professional gives an up-to-date and realistic picture of the roof’s condition.

Meeting your legal responsibility
The property owner is always responsible for roof safety: snow guards and safe access (walkways, ladders and fixings). Safety can only be verified on the roof itself by mechanical testing.
