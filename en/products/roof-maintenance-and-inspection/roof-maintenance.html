Roof maintenance

Maintenance services help keep your roof in working order year after year. Maintenance can often be a one-off job, seasonal maintenance or regular annual maintenance.

During maintenance, we clear loose debris from the roof, gutters and roof drains, check the general condition of the roof and document everything we find.

What does maintenance include?

Maintenance services include, for example:

  • Annual roof maintenance
  • Cleaning of gutters and roof drains
  • Mechanical cleaning of loose debris and growth from the roof
  • Treatment with a moss and algae control agent
  • Minor roof repairs
  • Sealing of penetrations, seams and seals
  • Replacement of roofing screws
  • Repair or replacement of edge flashings and small flashings
  • Replacement of broken roof tiles

Why order roof maintenance?

Avoid surprises
A roof can look fine from the ground even when damage is already starting. An inspection reveals problems in good time, before they lead to bigger costs.

Extend the life of your roof
Every roof eventually reaches the end of its life, but regular monitoring and preventive maintenance can postpone the need for a roof renovation by years.

Ensure roof safety
The property owner is responsible for making sure it is safe to move about on the roof. That is why snow guards, ladders, roof walkways and safety rope anchor points must also be inspected regularly. It is particularly important to inspect products that are used as safety rope anchor points.

Get a clear report
Every inspection and maintenance visit is documented thoroughly with photos. You can see what was found on the roof, what was done and what we may recommend next. That way you know exactly what condition your roof is in!
