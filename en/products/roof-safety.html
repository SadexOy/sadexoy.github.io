Roof safety products

We build safe, stable access routes on your roof that meet the regulations, for example for chimney maintenance or clearing snow. We also design and install correctly sized snow guards that protect against injury and damage to property. We install snow guards according to the snow load table of the RT card (in Finnish) – this ensures snow guards that match the load-bearing capacity of your roof and withstand even the snowiest winters.

Our Finnish-made roof safety products suit every roofing material on detached houses, apartment buildings and industrial buildings alike, and they have been tested to meet all corrosion and safety requirements.

When are roof safety products needed?

Roof safety products are needed so that moving and working on the roof is safe and easy. Under the regulations, buildings must have fixed roof safety products, and every part of the roof that needs maintenance must be reachable along fixed access routes. The building control authority requires roof safety products to be in place by the final inspection before the building is occupied.

When safety equipment is designed and built, the starting point is the architectural features and special characteristics of the building and its roofscape, which the equipment must not spoil. When roof safety equipment is added to old buildings, it is designed so that historically or architecturally valuable buildings and the townscape are not damaged. Building or altering ladders, roof walkways, guardrails and other equipment may change the appearance of the building and may therefore require a permit. Read more about the regulations for roof safety products (in Finnish).

Did the chimney sweep refuse to go up on the roof?

Professionals who carry out roof maintenance must also be guaranteed safe working conditions on the roof (in Finnish). If the roof safety equipment is not up to date, they have the right to refuse to go up on the roof. We deliver a complete solution that meets the regulations and serves residents and professionals alike.

Get your roof safety in order with these tips

Have your snow guards come loose?

If your snow guards have come loose, we recommend calling in a professional to check the roof safety system. In future it is important to make sure that the snow guards are installed according to the snow load table. Snow guards almost always come loose because the snow load exceeds their load-bearing capacity. Snow falling from the roof can then damage the snow guards and the roof sheeting, exposing the roof structures to moisture.

Read more on the Sadex blog (in Finnish)

Considering solar panels? Remember roof safety when planning their layout

Solar panels are often installed on a roof that already has snow guards, penetrations or other structures. People also often want to maximise the panel area, but at the same time it is important to leave enough space for the roof safety solutions and the maintenance route. By law, safe access to the parts of the roof that need maintenance must be guaranteed, and this must also be taken into account when installing panels. Installing solar panels must not reduce roof safety.

Bad ideas when it comes to roof safety

Old detached houses in particular, built before the 1990s, have many roof safety risks. Roof safety regulations have become stricter, and hidden hazards are worth fixing. If the roof has wooden ladders, old ladders or any of the things listed below, we recommend having the building’s roof safety system inspected.

  1. Movable roof ladders always pose a risk.
  2. Do not install a roof ladder over the ridge. It is not recommended.
  3. Wooden roof ladders or roof walkways should be replaced.
  4. Do not even consider tying a rope around the chimney or using other home-made anchor points!

Read more on the Sadex blog (in Finnish)

Wooden roof ladders are a safety risk

Today, wood does not meet the safety requirements for roof ladders. The purpose of the regulations is to ensure safe access to the roof, because climbing to high places is always risky. In the work of chimney sweeps and snow clearers, for example, the safety of every rung is extremely important so that no occupational safety risks arise.

If the roof has wooden ladders, they are either too old or installed against the recommendations. In both cases, wooden ladders are dangerous to use.

Fall protection on a housing company’s roof

Professionals know the right working methods, but it is the housing company’s responsibility to make sure the roof structures are safe for everyone who has to go up on the roof. Roof fall protection is meant to stop people from falling off the roof and at the same time to absorb the force of a fall.

A housing company’s roof fall protection is always designed and built for the specific property, depending for example on the height, pitch and material of the roof.

Is it worth installing roof safety products yourself?

People usually install roof safety products and gutters themselves to save costs. If you study the products and the installation instructions, you can do the job efficiently, especially on small buildings.

Installation costs vary with the building and the products. For a detached house, the installation typically accounts for about 500–800 euros. It is important to remember, however, that in renovation projects you are not entitled to the Finnish household tax credit (kotitalousvähennys) if you install the products on the roof yourself.

A professional installation is the worry-free option, because you can be sure of products that suit your roof, are correctly sized and meet the regulations. You also save valuable time. The professional takes responsibility for the whole installation and spots anything that needs repairing.

Roof work can be done in winter

Rain, snow and cold do not usually stop a professional from working on the roof. Very cold spells that make installation have to be postponed are short and rare these days.

Because of the weather, installing roof safety products and gutters may still need more preparation, but that is part of working outdoors. If your roof is under a layer of snow, for example, the snow is cleared before installation.
