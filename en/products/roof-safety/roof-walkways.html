Roof walkways

The most popular roof walkway on the market! Our durable, sturdy roof walkway lets snow through well and ensures safe access on the roof. The anti-slip features of the walkway grating and its excellent snow permeability of over 50% ensure stable footing even in slippery and difficult conditions. Thanks to adjustable feet, the roof walkway can be installed on all roof types and pitches. Our roof walkways are CE-marked and have passed demanding static and dynamic tests under VTT supervision.

Roof walkways are used as extensions of wall ladders and roof ladders to create safe access routes to service points on the roof, such as the chimney, and for attaching personal protective equipment. Manufactured by the Finnish company Piristeel Oy.

Material

Roof walkway components are made from hot-dip galvanised steel. The walkways are powder coated. Hot-dip galvanised steel and powder coating give the roof walkway excellent corrosion protection. Since 07/2013, roof walkways may no longer be manufactured with a galvanised-only finish, because in that form they do not meet the corrosion requirement for CE marking.

Colour

Sadex’s exceptionally wide range of standard colours ensures a roof walkway that suits the facade of your home, at no extra cost. Our standard colours for roof walkways are:

  • RR11 pine green
  • RR21 light grey
  • RR22 grey
  • RR23 dark grey
  • RR2H3 anthracite
  • RR32 dark brown
  • RR33 black
  • RR29 red
  • RR750 brick red

Roof walkways are also available in special colours at extra cost.

Technical data

  • Clear width 350 mm
  • Edge height 20 mm
  • Length 3000 mm
  • 1.25 mm hot-dip galvanised steel
  • Fixings 2.5 mm hot-dip galvanised steel
