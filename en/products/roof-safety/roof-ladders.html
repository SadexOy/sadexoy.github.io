Roof ladders

Roof ladders are an essential part of a property’s roof safety, ensuring safe access to the roof for maintenance and rescue situations. Our roof ladders are designed to withstand Finland’s changeable weather conditions, and they meet all official regulations. High-quality materials and professional installation ensure that the ladders stay safe and usable for a long time.

The Finnish-made SafeGrip roof ladder offers the best grip on the market for roof maintenance work. Compared with a conventional roof ladder with smooth rungs, its grip has been tested to be up to 100% better (VTT comparison report VTT-S-02719-18). The SafeGrip roof ladder features patented roughened rungs, which have been shown by research to reduce the risk of slipping, providing maximum occupational safety. The roughened rung tube grips the shoe and is comfortable to hold, so climbing works well even without gloves.

Besides SafeGrip and smooth-rung ladders, the market also offers so-called studded-rung roof ladders, where the rung has been roughened with added bumps. In wet conditions, however, a roof ladder with such bumps is even more slippery than a smooth-rung roof ladder – rather like a rain-soaked cobbled street, which is more slippery underfoot in the rain than smooth asphalt.

Installing roof ladders

SafeGrip roof ladders can be installed on all roofing materials. Roof ladders are fixed with brackets suited to each roof type and purpose. The SafeGrip roof ladder therefore provides safe access onto the roof and for moving on the roof to the ridge, the chimney, roof hatches and other points that need maintenance – on every roof.

Roof ladder prices

We supply CE-marked SafeGrip roof ladders for the price of ordinary smooth-rung ladders. SafeGrip roof ladders are made from strong, durable hot-dip galvanised steel tube and finished with Qualicoat 1 quality-approved powder coating, which gives the roof ladders excellent corrosion protection. Roof ladder materials and colours

Pisko SafeGrip roof ladders are made from 1.25 mm hot-dip galvanised steel. The ladders are powder coated. Hot-dip galvanised steel and powder coating give the roof ladders excellent corrosion protection. Atmospheric corrosivity category up to C4H.

Sadex’s exceptionally wide range of standard colours ensures roof ladders that suit the facade of your home, at no extra cost. The colour of roof ladders is usually chosen to match the colours of the roof. Our standard colours for roof ladders are:
