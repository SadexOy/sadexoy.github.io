Snow guards

Our snow guards make it safe to move about near buildings, protecting against the hazards that snow and ice falling from the roof can pose to people and property. Snow guards also protect structures on the roof, such as gutters and roof ladders.

Pisko SnowDefence Basic: 2-tube snow guard

Made in Finland, this durable, long-lasting 2-tube snow guard effectively prevents snow from falling off the roof and is suitable for all roofing types. Each roofing material has its own brackets, so the snow guard stays firmly and securely in place on any roof, even under large masses of snow.

Accessories available for the 2-tube snow guard include a groove stop and an additional profile for seamed roofs, which prevent even small amounts of snow and ice from falling. These accessories are popular and much in demand, especially with housing companies.

Pisko SnowDefence Premium: 3-tube snow guard

For more challenging roofs, the 3-tube snow guard, which is taller than a conventional snow guard, is the obvious choice. The 3-tube snow guard holds more snow on the roof and holds it better, and on steeper roofs the snow cannot easily slide over the guard. In addition, a sufficiently tall snow guard is recommended below solar panels, for example.

As an accessory for the 3-tube snow guard, a snow comb for seamed roofs is available; it effectively stops snow and ice sliding down the roof surface. The snow comb can also be retrofitted.

Pisko SnowDefence 150 snow fence

The Pisko SnowDefence 150 Compact snow fence is stylish and sturdy, and its size makes it particularly suitable for detached houses. In terms of appearance, the snow fence is also a serious alternative to the 2-tube snow guard. Functionally, the snow fence is equivalent to the 3-tube snow guard; the only significant difference between the products is their appearance. The snow fence can also be fitted with a snow comb. If you are looking for the safest snow guard option, we recommend either the 3-tube snow guard or the snow fence fitted with a snow comb.

Pisko SnowDefence 200 Heavy snow fence

Pisko SnowDefence 200 Heavy is the most effective and heavy-duty snow fence model, intended particularly for tall and large buildings. We recommend the 200 Heavy snow fence when maximum performance is required of the snow guard, for example to prevent snow and ice from falling off the roofs of tall buildings in built-up areas. The 200 Heavy snow fence also has an integrated ice stop at the bottom edge of the profile, which prevents snow and ice from sliding down the roof.

Pisko SnowDefence 300 Solar snow fence for solar panels

Pisko SnowDefence 300 Solar is designed especially for roofs with solar panels installed over a large area. Solar panels change the way a roof behaves, and their installation must be taken into account in the snow guards and other roof safety equipment. When the aim is to make maximum use of the panel area, there is often too little space between the eaves and the panels for a conventional snow guard. Solar panels also raise the snow load higher above the roof surface, so a sufficiently tall snow guard is needed to prevent the mass of snow from falling off the roof.

Pisko SnowDefence 300 Solar has been developed for exactly this purpose – the snow fence is tall enough and has an integrated ice stop at the bottom edge of the profile, making it the perfect solution for roofs with solar panels. The snow fence is suitable for standing-seam roofing, and it meets the recommendations of the RT card on combining solar panels and snow guards.

Price of snow guards installed

The price of a 2-tube snow guard installed on the roof of a detached house is on average approx. €950 – depending on the length of the guard and the bracket spacing, i.e. the number of brackets needed. Besides the bracket spacing, the price is also affected by the size of the site, the need for a lift, the need for additional battens and the distance of the site from our branch. The final price is always assessed site by site. We always recommend installing snow guards along the full length of the roof slope.

Material

Snow guard components are made from hot-dip galvanised steel. The guards are powder coated. Hot-dip galvanised steel and powder coating give the snow guard excellent corrosion protection. The snow guards are manufactured by Piristeel Oy.

Correct dimensioning of snow guards

Correct dimensioning of snow guards is extremely important for protecting both the roof and people. A snow guard that is too short and installed only above the walkway has to bear the weight of the snow that builds up on the entire roof slope, in which case even a high-quality snow guard can come loose and cause damage.
